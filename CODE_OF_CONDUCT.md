# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Contributors should communicate constructively, assume good intent while remaining factual, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving specific and actionable technical feedback;
- respecting different backgrounds, experiences, and accessibility needs;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes openly;
- protecting private data shared during support or debugging.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, or sexualized language and imagery.

## Scope

This standard applies in project spaces and whenever someone publicly represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's configured private contact channel. If no private channel is currently published, do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and respond proportionately.
